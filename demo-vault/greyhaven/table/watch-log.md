## Three days at the north gate

A **journal** is a log kept in the vault rather than in the chat panel. It is an
ordinary page with an ordinary `kind:`, so it renders, it links, it is indexed, and it
can be searched — and it is written by a person rather than assembled from a
transcript, which is the trade. The chat log on the Table is the live one; this is the
one that outlasts the evening, the one you can link to, and the one you can put a
collapsed secret callout in.

It is shaped like an export from the chat panel on purpose, because that is how these
actually get written: a speaker and a timestamp per line, and nothing clever.

---

**Day 1 · dusk · Halric, Warden.** At the gate. The Warden on the wall would not lift
the bar for a stranger without the miller's word, and the miller has not been seen
since the fire went out.

**Day 1 · dusk · Halric, Warden.** Told them the road is open to the mill and no
further. True as far as it goes.

**Day 1 · night · Sefa, Warden.** Counted the north road twice. Two sets of tracks
behind the mill, and a third heading *away* from town. Nothing on the road that would
have made them.

**Day 2 · morning · Sefa, Warden.** The eastern signal has not been burning for three
nights. The ash is cold and the oil is gone, and nobody at the gate will say whose
watch it was.

> [!secret]- What Sefa actually found at the mill  ^mill-shed
> The third set of tracks comes out of the mill shed, and the shed has a second door
> on the north face that is not on any plan of Greyhaven. Sefa has not told the other
> two. She wants to look again alone, at dawn, before she says anything at all.

**Day 2 · noon · Halric, Warden.** Told the party the fire is out and that we do not
yet know whose watch it was. Nothing about the shed.

**Day 3 · morning · Sefa, Warden.** A mirror hound came out of the heather east of
the road and would not cross the running water. It went back north along the bank
rather than around it.

**Day 3 · noon · Halric, Warden.** Went out to look. The standing stone east of the
gate has been opened — see [[north-road]] — and there is wax on the underside of it,
which is the only thing up here that anybody has bothered to seal in a long time.

---

## What this page demonstrates about search

The callout above is a **block quote**, so the rule that keeps secrets out of the
search index excludes the callout and nothing else. Everything else on this page —
including the line naming the fire — is searchable, and a search for "tracks" finds
Day 1 and Day 3.

The rule is blunt in the other direction, and this campaign pays for it in
[[secrets]]: a `[!secret]` marker that is **not** at the start of a block quote
excludes everything below it, because there is no marker saying where a secret stops
and guessing would be a leak waiting for a reveal. So on a page that documents the
syntax, the tail of the page is simply not in the search entry.

## What this page is not

It is not the audit trail. The audit trail records **disclosures** — who revealed a
callout, on which page, when — and lives in the database. It is not a chat export: the
Table's chat panel is a separate surface, and a line said at the table is not
automatically a line written down here. If your table wants its log searchable, the
journal is where it goes; if it wants it transcribed, something has to transcribe it.
