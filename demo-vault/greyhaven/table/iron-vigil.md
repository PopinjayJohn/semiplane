## One instance, standing on a road

A **token** is one instance of a game object. Not the rules for it, not a
character sheet — the thing standing on a map, at a position.

Iron Vigil is the Warden on the north road, and this page is what a Game Master writes
when a placement needs more than the four numbers the state carries. The state's own
fields are: a position, hit points and a maximum, a condition set, a visibility flag,
and a version. Everything else a table needs about a *particular* creature is prose,
and this is where it goes.

## Where it stands

- **Placement id** `gh-iron-vigil`, at 900, 620 on [[north-road]] — the road
  immediately north of the gate.
- **Hit points** 31 of 44. The map tints a placement from that fraction, in three
  bands, using colours that already mean something else in the shell.
- **Conditions** `concentration`, which is why the state holds a list and not a flag.
  `condition` is one of the five kinds semiplane owns, precisely so the tabletop can
  apply one without knowing what it *does*.
- **Visible** yes.

## A Warden

The class is [[warden]]. The feat it is currently spending a slot on is
[[steady-breath]], and the ancestry it carries is [[lantern-folk]].

Iron Vigil is running concentration on a spell the party has not seen the end of yet,
which is the kind of detail a page exists to record and a state document is the wrong
place for. The state holds what every client must agree on and what changes as the
table moves; this page holds what a player will want read aloud.

## What goes here and what goes on the table

| Belongs on the table | Belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| position, hit points, conditions, visibility, version | who they are, what they want, what they know |
| changes when the table moves | changes when the story does |
| every client sees the same value | a player may not be entitled to read it |

A secret about a token belongs in a `[!secret]` callout on this page — see
[[secrets]] — **not** in a private wiki page. A page the players cannot open is
a page the players will ask why they cannot open, and it is a second copy of a fact
with none of the audit trail. The two variants of a page and the reveal byte already
solve the problem this campaign-wide.
