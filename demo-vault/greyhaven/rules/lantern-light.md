## The spell at the gate

A **spell**. Its kind comes from the rules plugin's data pack, which is the point: the
same Markdown page is prose in a build that does not ship 5e, and it does not fail,
and nothing warns anybody. See [[rules-content]].

Lantern-light is the spell the Wardens use to hold a gate, and the reason this campaign
has a north gate worth holding is that somebody is always casting it.

{{dice:1d20}}

That slot is a **request to the server**, not a number this page computed. A roll is
an intent: the server validates it, applies it, increments the version and broadcasts
the result, so every roll is in the audit trail and the client never computes one the
server did not see. See [[rolling]] for why the page cannot render a total itself.

## What it does

*A soft steady light fills a 20-foot radius, and the light cannot be put out by wind or
rain.* It is not bright. It is the only light anyone trusts, because it does not go out
on the fourth night.

Casting it takes concentration, which is what [[steady-breath]] is for, and what makes
the choice at the gate a real one: hold the light and hold the wall, and say nothing
for as long as it takes.

## What the party knows

Nothing about the fourth night yet. The saving throw is what they will roll the first
time somebody tries to snuff it, and it is the first roll in this campaign that the
Table will put on a log.

## What this page is not

It is not a rules reference. It is the Game Master's page: the house reading, the
running notes, and the line about what the party has worked out. The pack holds the
spell's actual mechanics for every player in every campaign, and duplicating them here
would be a second answer to one question — see [[mirror-hound]] for the same argument
about a creature.
