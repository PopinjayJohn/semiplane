## Before the campaign started

A **background**: what a character did before the campaign started. It is the one kind
that is entirely a Game Master's content — no system ships a meaningful one, because a
background is a decision about a person rather than a rule.

Iron Vigil was a soldier. So was the gate Warden. So, by the shape of the questions
she asks, is Sefa.

## Why it is a kind at all

Because "who were you before this" is a different *sort* of page from "what can you
do". A class page is close to a reference; a background page is close to a
conversation. Putting them under one kind would lose the distinction; giving the pack
a dozen kinds for them would make the pack own prose it has no business writing.

So the pack declares `background` as a kind, the vault supplies the content, and the
page below is a Game Master's notes.

## Iron Vigil's

Six years on the south road, which is a road Greyhaven does not talk about, holding a
wall she has never seen the other side of. She came home when the posting closed and
took the north gate because it was the only post left.

- She will not say what was on the south road and she will not be asked twice.
- She considers refusing a watch a kind of cowardice, which is why
  [[steady-breath]] is a feat every Warden here takes rather than an option.
- She has not been home in four years and writes to nobody.

## What the party can pull out of it

This is the page's job: it holds what a character would say if asked the right
question, so that the Game Master does not have to invent it between evenings. The
question that works is not *what is your background* but *what did you hold, and for
whom* — and the answer is a wall, and somebody else's chain of command, and a road
nobody at the north gate will name.
