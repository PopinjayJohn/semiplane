## Hot flour

An **item**. Consumables, weapons, treasure and quest objects are all `item` to the
plugin, and the distinction between them is the **pack's** business rather than the
vault's. This page exists to make that concrete: semiplane has no opinion about what
makes a thing an item, and the pack's list is the only answer.

{{statblock:mending-draught}}

## What it does

Restores 2d4 + 2 hit points when drunk, and tastes of hot flour.

The second sentence is the important one for a wiki. It is not in the pack, and it is
not going to be — the pack is shared by every campaign shipping 5e, and "tastes of hot
flour" is a fact about Greyhaven's miller and about nothing else on the table.

## What this table is doing with it

The miller has not sold one in three days, which is longer than he has ever gone
without selling one. [[millers-letter]] is what he says about why, and the collapsed
callout on that page is what he does not say. The party has one draught and has not
spent it on [[iron-vigil]], who is at 31 of 44 and holding.

That is the whole reason the two pages exist as pages: the numbers live on the Table,
the situation lives in the vault, and the connection between them is a reader's job
rather than the product's.

## Why one kind for all of it

Because a kind is a **key**, and a key with a hierarchy is a vocabulary with two
answers. If the pack declared `potion` and `weapon` and `treasure` separately, a vault
page would have to know which axis it was writing on, and a campaign that wanted to add
"artefact" would have to ask semiplane to add a kind rather than adding one to its own
pack. One kind for the pack's objects, and the pack decides what they are.
