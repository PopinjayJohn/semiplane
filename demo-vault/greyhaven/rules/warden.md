## The class that holds a wall

A **class**. The kind is the plugin's, so a page carrying it is prose in a build that
does not ship 5e — which is the honest answer rather than a failure, and is the same
thing that happens to a page whose `kind:` is misspelt. See [[rules-content]].

A Warden is the town's guard, and the class exists because Greyhaven needs one that
does not swing a sword for a living. It is a class of people who hold a wall.

## What the class is for at this table

The Wardens are the party's employers and, increasingly, its suspects. They hold the
wall, they keep the gate, and they have been standing at both for three generations
without once writing down what happens on the far side of it. A class page is where
that stops being atmosphere:

- **Who the party answers to.** The gate Warden, [[iron-vigil]]'s counterpart at the
  north gate, who will not lift the bar without the miller's word.
- **What a promotion would cost.** The Wardens do not fight for coin. They fight for
  the post, and the post is theirs to lose.
- **What the class cannot do.** Nothing in the pack makes a Warden a good choice for a
  party that wants to leave town. That is a Game Master's decision and it belongs here.

## Features

Progression lives in the plugin's data. House variants of a feature belong on this
page, in prose, and belong to the Game Master:

- At second level a Warden may take [[steady-breath]], which every Warden at this
  table has and which is the reason they can hold a gate for a night.
- The class's saving-throw proficiency is one of the **house-rule settings** a campaign
  may change, and Greyhaven changes none — see [[house-rules]].

## What this page is not

It is not a character sheet. Sheets are not a thing semiplane has: a character is a
set of placements on a table plus whatever prose a Game Master writes, and the two are
deliberately not joined. See [[game-objects]] for why.
