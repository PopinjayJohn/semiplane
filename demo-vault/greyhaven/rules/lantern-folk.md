## Carrying a light that is not yours

An **ancestry**. Greyhaven needs one and the pack supplies it, and the reason the
vocabulary is not semiplane's is §10.2.1's: two systems must be able to declare an
ancestry each without colliding with the other's, because a system that could not
describe its own people would have to borrow another game's words for them.

Greyhaven's ancestry is about **carrying a light that is not yours** — a lantern kept
lit at the gate through the night, handed to whoever is on watch, never put down. Every
family here has one. Nobody can say when it started.

## What it gives

- You do not flinch at a fire that is out. You have seen one out.
- You can tell, by smell, whether a lantern has been left burning on oil or on
  something else.
- You have a place at a fire that is not yours, and you may claim it.

The third is the one that matters at this table, and it is the one the party has not
used yet.

## What it costs

- You cannot refuse to take a watch. Not as a rule — as a fact about the town, which
  is worse, because nobody has to enforce it.
- A lantern-folk who lets their light out is not asked to explain it. The town decides
  what happened, and the town decides quickly.

## And the miller

The miller is not a lantern-folk; his family has kept the mill for three generations
and the mill has no lantern in it. He is the only person at the north gate who is not
carrying one, and it has taken [[watch-log]]'s party three days to notice.

## The callout, and what you can check about it

The callout below is the mechanism; [[secrets]] explains it. Two things about it are
worth checking rather than believing:

- Its words are **not in this page's search entry**, in this state or in any other.
  A search for "lantern" finds this page — for the ancestry, for the cost, for the
  miller — and does not find the answer.
- The whole of this page is searchable, so the absence is the callout's doing and not
  the page's. Which means it is a claim you can falsify in two requests, and a vault
  that got it wrong would be wrong visibly.
